Asymflation

Field: Macroeconomics | JEL Codes: D40, E31, E52

Definition

Asymmetric inflation dynamics where prices rise rapidly in response to cost shocks but decline slowly or incompletely when costs fall, creating persistent upward pressure on the aggregate price level through downward price rigidities and behavioral asymmetries in price adjustment.

Etymology

Portmanteau of "asymmetric" and "inflation." The term links asymmetric price adjustment dynamics with inflation behavior and parallels established forms such as "stagflation" and "disinflation."

Usage Example

Energy markets displayed severe asymflation during 2024, with gasoline prices jumping 60% during supply shocks but declining only 7% when crude oil costs fell 19%, exemplifying the 'rockets and feathers' phenomenon in consumer markets.
