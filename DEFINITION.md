# Asymflation: Technical Definition

    ## Formal Definition
    Asymmetric inflation dynamics where prices rise rapidly in response to cost shocks but decline slowly or incompletely when costs fall, creating persistent upward pressure on the aggregate price level through downward price rigidities and behavioral asymmetries in price adjustment.

    ## Conceptual Background
    Asymflation is used here as an operational concept rather than a rhetorical label. It addresses asymmetric inflation dynamics under cost shocks. The mechanism focus is faster upward pass-through than downward adjustment in prices, which can be represented with explicit channels: input_cost_shock, price_increase_speed, price_decrease_speed, inflation_expectations, market_power, wage_rigidity, pass_through_gap.
